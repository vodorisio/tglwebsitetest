Benefits & Pay

Everything you need to thrive

As a TGL therapy traveler you're backed by a full package — malpractice coverage, continuing education, clinical mentorship, and more.

Malpractice Coverage

TGL MedStaff carries malpractice insurance for all travelers — so you can focus on delivering excellent patient care with confidence and peace of mind throughout your assignment.

CEU Reimbursement

Therapy travelers are eligible for up to $350 per year in continuing education reimbursement to maintain your licensure and grow your skills.

*Eligible once one 13-week assignment is completed and on a subsequent 13-week assignment or extension.

24/7 Clinical Support & Mentorship

Our therapy division has on-staff therapy support available around the clock for clinical check-ins, guidance, and mentorship — so you always have a clinical colleague in your corner.

Licensure Reimbursement

Travelers may be eligible for reimbursement of licensing fees and required certifications for their assignment.

Health & Life Insurance

Insurance options including health, dental, and life coverage to protect you and your family on the road.

Referral & Loyalty Bonuses

Earn up to $750 when a referred traveler completes a 13-week assignment. Repeat travelers also earn Good Life loyalty bonuses.

*Referral bonus amount may vary based on discipline and specialty. Ask your recruiter for details.

New Grad Travelers

Just graduated?
Travel is an option.

Therapy is unique among travel healthcare disciplines — it is one of the few fields where new graduates are routinely accepted for travel assignments. If you just finished school and are wondering whether travel could be your first step, the answer might be yes.

Travel as a new grad can accelerate your clinical development, expose you to diverse settings faster than most perm jobs, and give you the freedom to explore the country while you figure out where you want to land long-term.

At TGL we love working with new grads. Asking the right questions up front — about setting preferences, support needs, and what a great first assignment looks like — is how we make sure we find the right fit for you.

Talk to a Clinician

Is travel right for you as a new grad?

Our therapy team has walked this path — we can help you think through the decision honestly and without pressure. Whether you're ready to go or just exploring, reach out and start the conversation.
