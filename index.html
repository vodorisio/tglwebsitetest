Core Values

How we show up every day

CONNECT

We build real relationships — with our travelers, our facilities, and each other. Every connection we make is intentional, personal, and built to last beyond a single assignment.

CARE

We genuinely care about the wellbeing of our clinicians and the patients they serve. That means being available, responsive, and invested — not just during the placement, but throughout the journey.

COMPETE

We hold ourselves to the highest standard in the industry. Competitive pay, strong benefits, responsive service — we compete every day to make TGL the best place to travel and the best partner for facilities.

Our Mission

Quality care,
delivered everywhere.

Our highest priority is quality patient care, the wellbeing of our traveling clinicians, and meeting the unique needs of our partner healthcare facilities. We are committed to ensuring our partner facilities have the quality staff they need to provide top-notch care.

We are committed to helping our traveling clinicians identify and secure opportunities that fulfill their desire for adventure as well as their passion for their work. We believe the good life includes access to quality healthcare — and we are committed to doing our part to facilitate the movement of resources to areas of highest need.

Who we serve
Travel nurses, allied health professionals, therapy travelers, locum physicians — and the facilities that need them most.
Our commitment
Ensuring quality staff reach facilities in areas of greatest need, from urban medical centers to rural critical access hospitals.
Our promise
At TGL MedStaff, you are more than an employee or a client — you are family. We create positive experiences over and over again.
