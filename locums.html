What We Offer

Full-service locum
tenens support

From your first conversation to your first shift, we handle the details so you can focus on patient care. Our full-service approach is designed to make locum work seamless, rewarding, and professionally flexible.

Credentialing & Licensing

Our in-house credentialing team manages your applications, primary source verifications, and state license acquisition from start to finish.

Dedicated Recruiter

You'll work with one recruiter who learns your preferences, negotiates on your behalf, and stays your single point of contact throughout.

Housing & Travel

We coordinate housing and travel arrangements for your locum opportunity, allowing providers to focus on patient care while we handle the logistics. From arrival to departure, our team works to make each assignment seamless and stress-free.

Competitive Compensation

We offer competitive pay, weekly direct deposits, and malpractice coverage to ensure locum tenens work is both financially rewarding and professionally flexible.

Malpractice Coverage

Occurrence-based malpractice insurance included with every locum shift, so you're protected at every facility you serve.

Flexible Scheduling

Short-term gaps, long-term contracts, or on-call coverage — we match you with assignments that fit your schedule.
