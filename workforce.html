What We Offer

End-to-end workforce
partnership

We don't just fill shifts — we build strategic staffing programs that give your facility control, consistency, and cost savings.

Managed Service Provider (MSP)

We become the single point of accountability for your entire contingent workforce — recruiting, onboarding, compliance, timekeeping, and reporting under one roof.

24/7 Support

Our team is available around the clock — because staffing challenges don't follow business hours. Day or night, you have a real person ready to respond.

Qualified Candidates

Direct access to TGL's network of qualified healthcare providers and allied health professionals — faster fill rates, pre-screened candidates, and competitive bill rates.

Compliance & Credentialing

Every traveler arrives fully credentialed and compliant. We manage licensure, background checks, immunizations, and ongoing compliance tracking so you don't have to.

Workforce Analytics

Data-driven reporting on fill rates, cost-per-hire, tenure, and agency performance — giving your leadership team the insight to make smarter staffing decisions.

On-Staff Clinical Team

Our in-house clinical team is available to every traveler for coaching, guidance, and support throughout their assignment — so your facility benefits from providers who feel backed and perform at their best.

Program Models

We fit your model

Whether you need a full MSP overhaul or targeted healthcare provider support, we tailor the engagement to your facility's size, budget, and goals.

Total Workforce Management

TGL assumes full management of your contingent labor program — serving as your MSP, handling all vendor relationships, compliance, and reporting end-to-end.

Preferred Vendor Partnership

We integrate alongside your existing vendors as a preferred supplier — filling specialty gaps, providing surge capacity, and improving your overall fill rate and time-to-fill.

On-Demand Traveler Access

Access our vetted pool of healthcare providers and allied health professionals directly — fast submissions, competitive rates, zero administrative overhead.

How It Works

Up and running in 4 steps

From discovery call to fully staffed units — our onboarding is designed to be fast, clear, and low-lift for your team.

1

Discovery Call

We learn your facility's current staffing gaps, vendor setup, budget constraints, and workforce goals.

2

Program Design

We propose a tailored program model — MSP, hybrid, or direct fill — with clear timelines and cost projections.

3

Onboarding & Integration

We set up systems, introduce your account manager, and begin submitting qualified travelers within days.

4

Ongoing Partnership

Regular reporting, performance reviews, and proactive planning keep your units staffed and your program optimized.
